# AI News Drop

**Wednesday, July 15, 2026**

The theme today: AI getting smaller, faster, and more capable all at once — while the infrastructure holding it all together starts to show strain.

* * *

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**A 27B Model Fits in Your Pocket**

[PrismML's Bonsai 27B](https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-27b) just landed on Hacker News with 634 points and counting — and for good reason. It's the first 27B-class model to run on a phone, and the engineering behind it is sharp: 1-bit quantization shrinks the model from 54GB down to 3.8GB (a 93% reduction) while retaining meaningful capability. That's not a research demo. That's a shipping artifact.

Why it matters: on-device models at this scale fundamentally change the privacy and latency calculus for mobile AI apps. No API call, no data leaving the device, no inference costs. For health, finance, or any category where users are rightly paranoid about data, this is a real unlock. The open-source community is already [spinning up](https://x.com/huggingface/status/2077244214341980557).

Watch for: whether Bonsai 27B's quality holds on longer-context tasks, and how Apple responds with their own on-device model roadmap ahead of the fall hardware cycle.

* * *

**Cursor Has a 0-Day — And Full Disclosure Is the Only Fix**

The [Mindgard writeup](https://mindgard.ai/blog/cursor-0day-when-full-disclosure-becomes-the-only-protection-left) on a Cursor zero-day is sitting at 397 points on HN with 187 comments. The short version: there's an insecure handling vulnerability serious enough that the researchers went straight to full public disclosure after the coordinated process stalled. For a tool that now lives inside millions of developers' codebases, the blast radius matters.

This is the second major AI coding tool security story in a week (Codex's prompt encryption issue was covered Monday). The pattern is real: as these tools get deeper access to repos, terminals, and credentials, the attack surface expands accordingly. If you're running Cursor in a team environment with production credentials anywhere in scope, read the post before your next standup.

### 📡 Quick Hits

**Sam Altman flags scaling friction ahead.** With GPT-5.6 Sol hitting what Altman called ["5.6 sol growth"](https://x.com/sama/status/2077106587307798989) — framed as "insane" — he's also warning that infrastructure may hiccup under demand. Translation: OpenAI's inference team is running hot, and degraded availability is possible soon. Plan accordingly if you have production dependencies on the API.

**Meta AI aces the Asian Physics Olympiad.** [Meta's AI](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2077138553210028042) scored a perfect 30/30 on the theoretical exam, tying with the top 3 human student contestants. It's a targeted benchmark, not a general test — but it's a meaningful data point on frontier reasoning at a non-OpenAI/Anthropic lab.

**Grok 4.5 claims #2 on FrontierSWE.** Per [an account tracking the benchmark](https://x.com/cb_doge/status/2077367712087671249), Grok 4.5 is now ranked second on FrontierSWE, ahead of Claude Opus 4.8. xAI is clearly pushing hard on coding capability. Take leaderboard claims with the usual grain of salt, but the trajectory is worth watching.

**OpenAI Codex nears 9 million users.** Usage is [reportedly surging](https://x.com/Amank1412/status/2077311896164987265) on the back of GPT-5.6. For context on how fast this market is moving: that's a meaningful installed base for a dev tool that didn't exist in its current form 18 months ago.
