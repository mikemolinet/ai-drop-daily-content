**The July 2026 Frontier Lab Agent Intrusion — Fully Dissected**

Hugging Face just published a detailed technical timeline of the agent intrusion incident from earlier this month, and it's getting serious traction on Hacker News (414 pts, 224 comments). Why this matters beyond the drama: the incident is quickly becoming a reference case for how _not_ to deploy agents with persistent credentials and broad permissions. If you're shipping agents into production, this is required reading. **India Bets on a Trillion-Parameter Model Built at Home**

[Sarvam AI announced at Epoch 2026 in Bengaluru](https://x.com/beatsinbrief/status/2082804457029669332) that it's building a 1T+ parameter model trained from scratch in India on 4,000 Hopper GPUs — targeting coding, cybersecurity, scientific research, and simulations. Sam Altman also teased something adjacent: [he's "very close" to models that will significantly accelerate scientific discovery](https://x.com/sama/status/2082628413769003269), with an emphasis on empowering scientists rather than replacing them.

The Sarvam announcement is notable for what it signals: India isn't just consuming frontier AI, it's making a serious infrastructure bet to produce it domestically. Training a 1T+ parameter model at home is expensive and hard — but it's also sovereign. Watch for more national-scale model projects in 2026 as compute access democratizes.

**The Outsourcing Collapse Is Now in the Revenue Numbers**

[Chamath flagged](https://x.com/chamath/status/2082481943258529873) that Infosys cut its revenue forecast this week, citing enterprise budgets shifting from outsourced labor hours to AI tooling. This isn't a vibe — it's a line item in a public earnings report. The hourly billing model that built India's IT services industry was always dependent on work staying slow and manual. It didn't.

The transition is happening faster than most predicted. This is the macro backdrop for every "AI replaces X" conversation — and it's starting to show up in actual financials, not just forecasts.
