# AI News Drop — Thursday, July 16, 2026

The open-source model wars just escalated. A new 975B-parameter open-weights model dropped overnight, Grok Build went fully open source, and Sam Altman is warning that GPT-5.6's growth is so fast the wheels might wobble. Builders are eating good.

* * *

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**Thinking Machines Releases Inkling — 975B Parameters, Fully Open**

[Thinking Machines](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/introducing-inkling/) dropped Inkling today, a 975B open-weights model with image and audio support, 1M context, and an NVFP4 checkpoint available via Unsloth for local quantized runs. It's the biggest open-weights release in recent memory, and it's already the #1 story on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com) with 1,046 points and 260 comments. The model was trained on NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 hardware, and NVIDIA AI was quick to congratulate — this is a win for the ecosystem they're building.

Early verdict from Ethan Mollick: [rough around the edges](https://x.com/emollick/status/2077593908540850491). He ran the standard Lem Test and Inkling failed — something every frontier model since DeepSeek R1 and Sonnet 3.5 passes. So: historically large open-weights model, not yet frontier quality. That's still a significant gap, but the trajectory matters. If you're building on open models, this is worth watching and testing.

**Grok Build Goes Open Source**

[xAI open-sourced Grok Build](https://github.com/xai-org/grok-build) today — 489 points on HN with 534 comments, which is a lively thread. Combined with the extended limits on Claude Fable 5 and daily resets on GPT-5.6, the builder sentiment on X right now is genuinely euphoric. [Alex Finn captured it](https://x.com/AlexFinn/status/2077616606285578254): three major platforms simultaneously making their tools cheaper and more accessible is not a coincidence — it's competitive pressure doing exactly what it's supposed to do.

The "dirt cheap" framing on Grok Build is key. xAI is clearly trying to pull developers away from OpenAI and Anthropic on price. Watch the GitHub stars and early integrations over the next 48 hours to gauge real adoption vs. hype.

**Sam Altman Flags GPT-5.6 Scaling Strain**

Altman [posted](https://x.com/sama/status/2077106587307798989) that GPT-5.6's 5.6 SOL growth is "insane" and warned of possible "hiccups" ahead as they scramble to scale inference. Separately, OpenAI [introduced GPT-Red](https://x.com/sama/status/2077487858508820853), an internal automated red-teamer hunting prompt injection vulnerabilities at scale — a quiet but important safety infrastructure move.

The inference strain story is worth taking seriously. When Altman preemptively warns about outages, plan accordingly.

### 📡 Quick Hits

-   **NVIDIA's robot memory breakthrough:** Jim Fan's team at [NVIDIA](https://x.com/DrJimFan/status/2077414142340988962) scaled a robot policy model to 8,000 timesteps (5 full minutes) of context at constant inference cost. Robot policies used to forget everything after a fraction of a second. Three orders of magnitude improvement in contextual memory is not incremental — it's foundational for real-world robotics.

-   **Codex and Claude Code get multi-tab browsers:** Both tools now let agents [open multiple browser tabs natively inside the app](https://x.com/s1rozha_/status/2077716106538533252) — not external windows, not Chrome extensions. The browser as the AI operating system is becoming literal.

-   **Google DeepMind + Isomorphic Labs on bioresilience:** [DeepMind announced](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2077721122116640969) a partnership with Isomorphic Labs to deploy frontier AI for proactive biosecurity — outbreak detection before outbreaks happen. Quiet but high-stakes.

-   **ICML 2026 reproducibility problem:** [70% of AI research isn't reproducible](https://x.com/huggingface/status/2077657128223768647), and 6,000+ papers just dropped from ICML. AlphaXiv flagged this. The volume of research is outpacing our ability to verify it.

-   **YC founders are mostly at OpenAI and Anthropic:** A [new tracker](https://joinedanthropic.com) mapped where YC founders landed — the two labs are the dominant gravity wells for technical founders right now (109 pts on HN).
