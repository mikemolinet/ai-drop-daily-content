**OpenAI Enters Robotics Race, Nvidia Drops RTX Spark Chip**

Monday's looking like hardware meets intelligence — OpenAI just posted they're hiring for a full robotics team while Nvidia launched their RTX Spark chip to bring AI agents directly onto your laptop.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**OpenAI Goes Physical with Robotics Division**

Sam Altman [announced](https://x.com/sama/status/2061117302528188712) OpenAI is hiring "exceptional full-stack hardware, ops, systems, and ML engineers" for a new robotics division focused on "robots that are useful for society." The timing isn't coincidental — after conquering text, images, and code, the logical next frontier is the physical world. This puts OpenAI in direct competition with Figure, Boston Dynamics, and Tesla's robot ambitions. What's interesting: they're emphasizing "useful" over flashy, suggesting they're targeting practical applications rather than demo-worthy stunts. The hire-first announcement signals they're serious about building internal capabilities rather than just partnering with existing robotics companies.

**Nvidia RTX Spark Brings Local AI to Every Laptop**

Nvidia launched the RTX Spark chip in Taiwan, developed with Microsoft, designed to run AI agents locally rather than through cloud APIs. [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/) this is specifically targeting the shift toward personal AI agents that can work offline and keep your data private. The timing aligns with growing enterprise concerns about cloud-based AI security and the creator economy's push for local AI workflows. Expect this to accelerate the "AI PC" trend and potentially disrupt the cloud inference market if performance meets expectations.
