# AI News Drop — Tuesday, June 30, 2026

Brain-computer interfaces and a 1.6 trillion-parameter model from a food delivery app. Welcome to the last day of June.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**Meta's Brain2Qwerty v2 Is a Non-Invasive BCI Milestone**

Meta AI dropped the next version of its Brain2Qwerty research today — a non-invasive brain-to-text decoder that lets people type using only brain signals, no implants required. Building on v1, the new version improves accuracy and speed meaningfully enough that researchers and builders are paying attention. [The announcement](https://x.com/yoheinakajima/status/2071691797219381266) is getting wide traction across the AI community.

Why it matters: non-invasive BCI has always been the harder problem. Neuralink gets the headlines, but requiring surgery caps the addressable market at essentially zero for consumer applications. If Meta can push non-invasive accuracy into genuinely useful territory, they're playing a completely different game — one that could complement their AR/VR hardware roadmap. As [Craig Weiss put it](https://x.com/craigweiss/status/2071686677559644383): imagine if Meta differentiates from OpenAI and Anthropic not on models, but on becoming the leader in brain-computer interfaces. That's an unexpected moat.

Watch for: how this intersects with Meta's Ray-Ban smart glasses and Quest hardware. BCI as an input layer for spatial computing is the long play here.

* * *

**Meituan Built a 1.6T-Parameter Model — On 50,000 Domestic Chips**

China's food-delivery giant Meituan released a 1.6 trillion-parameter model this week, trained entirely on domestic Chinese chips. [LongCat-2.0](https://longcat.chat/blog/longcat-2.0/) (a large-scale MoE model with 1.6T total and 48B active parameters) is already on Hacker News with traction from the research community.

The headline stat isn't the parameter count — it's the chip story. Training a frontier-scale model on 50,000 domestic chips, without NVIDIA H100s or A100s, is a direct proof-of-concept that export controls have a ceiling. If a dinner-delivery company can do this, the "chip embargo as AI containment" thesis just took a hit. The MoE architecture (48B active out of 1.6T total) keeps inference costs sane, which is exactly what you'd need to actually deploy this at Meituan's scale.

Worth noting: this is the second major Chinese open-weight model to surface this week after GLM-5.2. The cadence is accelerating.

**Lucida AI Raises $7M to Go After Duolingo.** London-based [Lucida AI](https://techfundingnews.com/lucida-ai-7m-seed-duolingo-london/) raised $7M from Velocity Capital for a speech-first language learning platform.
