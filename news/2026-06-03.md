The enterprise AI honeymoon is cooling. Uber reportedly capped coding agents at $1,500 per employee per month, signaling companies are getting serious about ROI after months of unlimited AI experimentation.

## 🔥 The Big Stories

**Enterprise AI Gets Budget Reality Check**

Uber's $1,500 monthly cap on coding agents per employee marks a shift from the "try everything" phase to measured deployment. The move suggests Uber sees real value in these tools—$1,500/month is significant budget—but also wants guardrails. This could become the new normal as enterprises demand clearer productivity metrics from AI tools. [Watch for other Fortune 500s announcing similar caps](https://x.com/simonw/status/2062143151184465964).

**Factory AI Ships Model Router for Automatic Task Assignment**

Factory launched automatic model routing that picks the optimal AI model for each task without manual selection. The system maintains "frontier performance" while optimizing costs by routing simple tasks to cheaper models and complex work to premium ones. This addresses a real enterprise pain point—teams waste time model-shopping instead of building. Expect this to become table stakes for AI platforms.

**Visual AI Tools Generate Editable Source Code, Not Just Pixels**

A16z highlights how the most interesting visual AI tools now output the underlying code behind images and designs, not just final pixels. This unlocks editability and iteration loops that pixel-native models can't match. The shift toward code generation means visual AI becomes part of the development workflow, not just a design tool. [Markets are organizing around this approach](https://x.com/a16z/status/2061842081753297176).
