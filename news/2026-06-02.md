The AI IPO pipeline is getting crowded — and expensive. Anthropic just became the world's most valuable private AI company after closing a massive $65B funding round that values the ChatGPT rival at nearly $1 trillion. Meanwhile, builders are getting new tools as Crossmint launches payment APIs for AI agents and PewDiePie drops an open-source AI workspace.

## 🔥 The Big Stories

**Anthropic Hits $965B Valuation in Record Funding Round**

Anthropic closed a staggering [$65B funding round](https://aifundingtracker.com/) that pushes its post-money valuation to $965 billion, making it the most valuable private AI company globally and surpassing OpenAI's last reported valuation. The round comes as investors pile into AI companies ahead of what could be [three trillion-dollar IPOs this year](https://twitter.com/Amank1412/status/2061751946336424364) across Anthropic, OpenAI, and SpaceX.

This funding frenzy reflects the massive capital requirements for frontier AI development, but it's also creating pressure on earlier AI startups. A new [CNBC report](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/ai-startup-valuations-pre-chatgpt.html) warns that hundreds of pre-ChatGPT AI companies are being "disrupted or dead" as the $250B+ flowing to mega-rounds leaves smaller players struggling for relevance and funding.

**Alphabet Announces $80B Capital Raise for AI Infrastructure**

Google's parent company is [raising $80 billion](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/alphabet-plans-to-raise-80-billion-to-pay-for-ai-buildout/) to fund its massive AI infrastructure expansion. The equity raise signals just how expensive the AI arms race has become — even Google needs fresh capital to keep pace with compute demands. This follows the pattern of tech giants pouring unprecedented resources into AI capabilities.

-   **OpenAI Models Hit AWS**: OpenAI's frontier models and Codex are [now available on Amazon Web Services](https://openai.com/index/openai-frontier-models-and-codex-are-now-available-on-aws/), expanding access for enterprise developers building AI applications.

-   **Step 3.7 Flash Goes Free**: @StepFun\_ai dropped their [Step 3.7 Flash model](https://twitter.com/JasonVsTheNoise/status/2061773074505113695) on the Nous Portal with 30 days free access — being touted as the world's fastest open-source model.

-   **GitHub Moves to Usage-Based Billing**: As of June 1, [all Copilot plans now bill on GitHub AI Credits](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week) with usage-based pricing, marking a shift in how developers pay for AI coding assistance.

-   **Gemini 3.5 Pro Coming This Month**: Google's "MONSTER" [Gemini 3.5 Pro model](https://twitter.com/goodworse/status/2061718402398360027) is expected to drop in June, with the team claiming huge improvements over 3.1 Pro. Polymarket is offering 87% odds on a June release.

## 💡 Builder's Corner

**AI Agents Get Payment Rails**: Crossmint launched the [Agentic Cards API](https://twitter.com/crossmint/status/2061782578944962617), built with Visa Intelligent Commerce, enabling AI agents to make secure payments with Visa cards. This solves a key infrastructure gap for autonomous agent applications that need to handle transactions.

**PewDiePie's Open-Source AI Workspace**: The YouTuber released ["Odysseus"](https://twitter.com/pubity/status/2061764769745691111), a free self-hosted AI workspace. His reasoning: "The more you share of yourself with AI, the better it becomes. But the more you do that, the more you are handing a huge piece of yourself to all these giant tech companies." Worth checking out for privacy-conscious builders.
