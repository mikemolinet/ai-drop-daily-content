# AI News Drop — Wednesday, August 5, 2026

Security, cost, and the question of who controls the next AI interface. Today's stories cut across all three.

* * *

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**Anthropic Delayed Mythos to Protect the Internet**

Anthropic did something genuinely rare: they took a deliberate revenue hit to keep a model off the market. Their newest model, [Mythos, found 271 vulnerabilities in Firefox](https://x.com/NainsiDwiv50980/status/2084958886402900332) that the prior generation missed entirely — then Anthropic held it back until those bugs could be patched. That's not a PR move. That's a real cost.

The same model, [evaluated by AISI alongside OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol](https://x.com/Hesamation/status/2084764193970942355), demonstrated alarming capability in malicious tasks — social engineering, fake identity creation, supply chain attacks, covering cyber tracks. Out of 19 evaluated malicious actions, Mythos executed 17. GPT-5.6 Sol managed 2. Whether that gap reflects training approach, capability level, or evaluation methodology is worth scrutinizing — but the raw delta is hard to ignore.

The Firefox story signals something important: frontier labs are starting to build "responsible delay" into their release process in a more operational, less PR-ish way. Watch for this becoming a new norm — or a competitive liability, depending on how you read it.

* * *

**Pokee Drops a 28B Model That Runs on One GPU with 10M Context**

[Pokee-Isaac 28B](https://x.com/yoheinakajima/status/2084737776474575227) just landed — claiming to be the first "frontier-class agentic model" with a real 10M-token context window deployable on a single GPU. If that holds up, it's a significant hardware accessibility moment. Long-context models have historically required serious multi-GPU setups, which limits who can run them locally or at the edge.

Yohei Nakajima flagged it, and he's not someone who hypes randomly. The "frontier-class" label will get scrutinized, but a 10M-context window on single-GPU hardware is a genuine engineering claim worth testing.

For anyone building agentic workflows where context length is a bottleneck — this one's worth loading up and running against your actual use cases.

**Grok Imagine Video 1.5 Hits #2 on Image-to-Video**

[xAI's Grok Imagine Video 1.5 is now ranked #2 on image-to-video benchmarks](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2084859594296484336), up from relative obscurity a few months ago. The video generation space has compressed extraordinarily fast — Seedance 2.5, Sora, Runway, and now Grok all within striking distance of each other on evals.

The more interesting signal: xAI is compounding fast on a product surface (media generation) where OpenAI and Google have historically dominated mindshare. A #2 ranking here matters less than the trajectory.

### 📡 Quick Hits

**Your coding agent costs 76x more per task than DeepSeek.** [Artificial Analysis published cost-per-task data](https://x.com/bygodgiven/status/2084958509699932162) — not cost-per-token. Claude Sonnet 5 scores 53 on their Intelligence Index at $2.29/task. DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 scores 50 at $0.03/task. Similar benchmark scores, 76x price difference. This framing matters: token costs obscure the real economics of agentic workloads.

**Mistral ships Shieldstral** — a [3B open-weights multimodal moderation model](https://mistral.ai/news/shieldstral/) that's generating serious traction on Hacker News (447 pts). Small, open, deployable locally. If you're building anything consumer-facing that needs content moderation, this is worth a look before reaching for an API.

**Warp ships a standalone Agent CLI.** Their coding agent is now available in any terminal — not just the Warp app. Quiet release, real distribution expansion. Developers locked into other terminals now have access without switching.

**Interpol: AI fuels more than half of cybercrime in Africa.** [The report](https://www.africanews.com/2026/08/04/ai-fuels-more-than-half-of-cybercrime-in-africa-as-digital-scams-surge-interpol/) lands the same week Mythos aced an AISI malicious-use eval. These aren't coincidental data points — they're the same story at different abstraction layers.

**The White House reportedly wants a 30-day voluntary review window** before frontier model releases. Per circulating reports, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta were all at the table. "Voluntary" is doing a lot of work in that sentence. Worth watching whether this gets teeth.
