### 🔥 The Big Stories

**Perplexity Open-Sources Internal Developer Security Tool**

Perplexity surprised the AI community by [open-sourcing Bumblebee](https://x.com/dekrypthor/status/2058649349186306188), the security tool they use internally to keep their own developers safe. This isn't just another GitHub dump — it's the actual production system they've been using to prevent AI-powered security incidents. The move signals growing recognition that AI security tooling needs to be collaborative rather than competitive. Expect other AI companies to follow suit as the industry realizes security vulnerabilities affect everyone when models can be weaponized.

**Vatican Announces Partnership with Anthropic**

In a move that feels straight out of a sci-fi novel, [Pope Francis announced the Catholic Church will work with Anthropic](https://x.com/Vivek4real_/status/2058875709469430111) to "find the way for humanity in this time of AI." While details remain sparse, this represents the first major religious institution formally partnering with an AI company on existential questions. The timing isn't coincidental — as AI capabilities accelerate, religious leaders are recognizing they need to be part of the conversation about AI alignment and human values, not just observers.
