## 🔥 The Big Stories

**Pentagon's Anthropic Blacklisting Ruled Unlawful**

A U.S. judge [blocked the Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-judge-blocks-pentagons-anthropic-blacklisting-2026-08-28/), ruling it unlawful in a decision that landed today. The Defense Department had moved to restrict Anthropic from federal contracting — an unusual move against a major AI lab — and the judge found no legal basis for it.

This matters beyond Anthropic specifically. The ruling sets a precedent that executive agencies can't arbitrarily shut AI companies out of government work. With federal AI spending accelerating, being blacklisted from DoD contracts is essentially being cut out of one of the biggest procurement markets in the world. The decision reopens that door.

Watch for whether the Pentagon appeals, and whether this emboldens other labs to push back harder against agency-level restrictions. The legal infrastructure around government AI procurement is getting stress-tested in real time.

**Real-Time AI Video Is Here — H3 Max Breaks the Wall**

Ethan Mollick [documented something worth paying attention to](https://x.com/emollick/status/2093082102312923351): Runway's H3 Max is now generating reasonably high-quality AI video _faster than real-time_ — generation completes before you'd finish watching the output. He tested this via the standard web interface, no API tricks.

This is a genuine threshold crossing. "Fast enough to iterate" is a different product category than "wait 3 minutes per clip." When video generation becomes as fast as image generation, the production workflows around it change completely — for marketing, for games, for film pre-viz. The otter astronaut demo he posted is rough around the edges, but the _speed_ is the story.

**Over 100 Orgs Sign Open Cyber Defense Letter — Sam Altman Urges Urgency**

Sam Altman [retweeted and co-signed](https://x.com/sama/status/2093058937935241416) an open letter calling for a global surge in AI-enabled cyber defense, with signatories including Anthropic, AWS, Google, and Microsoft. Altman's [own framing](https://x.com/sama/status/2093060670472241368): "there is not much time to act" and "only an urgent and intense collective response will work."

The rare cross-industry alignment here — OpenAI and its competitors signing the same letter — signals this isn't PR. The subtext is that offensive AI cyber capabilities are outpacing defensive ones, and the window to close the gap is narrowing. If you're building anything in security, this is the macro tailwind of the moment.
