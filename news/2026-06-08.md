Two massive funding rounds are reshaping China's AI landscape this week, with DeepSeek reportedly close to raising $7.4 billion and Moonshot AI seeking up to $2 billion at a $30 billion valuation.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**DeepSeek's $7.4B War Chest Targets Global Competition**

DeepSeek is nearing completion of a 50 billion yuan ($7.4 billion) funding round—its first major external raise. The timing isn't coincidental: this comes just days after [DeepSeek V4 Pro reportedly outperformed GPT-5.5 Pro on precision benchmarks](https://runtimewire.com/article/deepseek-v4-pro-beats-gpt-5-5-pro-on-precision), suggesting the Chinese lab is positioning for a serious challenge to Western AI dominance. The scale here matters—$7.4B would rank among the largest AI funding rounds globally, giving DeepSeek resources to compete on compute, talent, and global expansion.

**Moonshot AI Swings for $30B Valuation**

Moonshot AI, known for its Kimi chatbot, is targeting a $30 billion valuation in talks for up to $2 billion in new funding. The company has been quietly building a consumer AI assistant that's gained traction in Chinese markets. At $30B, Moonshot would be valued higher than many established tech companies—a sign that investors see massive potential in AI assistants that can break through in non-English markets.

**Block Open-Sources Goose: Enterprise-Grade AI Coding Agent**

Jack Dorsey's Block just [open-sourced Goose](https://x.com/_0xpainn/status/2063950480791818456), their internal AI coding agent that was built for production use. Unlike many coding assistants, Goose reads entire codebases, edits files autonomously, runs commands, and fixes bugs while supporting any model. The fact that Block is giving away their internal tooling suggests they see strategic value in establishing Goose as a standard—potentially positioning Block as infrastructure for the AI coding wave.

### 📡 Quick Hits

**Anthropic Ships Terminal Tool "ant"** — Anthropic quietly [released a command-line tool called "ant"](https://x.com/VK_ROXy/status/2063958892178973118) on June 2nd that gives developers direct terminal access to every Claude API endpoint without JSON formatting or header management.

**YC Launches Paxel for Founder Signal** — Y Combinator [launched Paxel](https://x.com/rrhoover/status/2063323917528227901), which Ryan Hoover calls "a smart way to get signal on who's legit"—though details on what Paxel actually does remain sparse.

**Anthropic Releases 13 Free AI Courses** — The company [dropped a full curriculum](https://x.com/HeyAmit_/status/2063950490858397782) of free AI courses with certificates, including Claude 101 and Claude Code 101, as competition heats up for developer mindshare.

### 💡 Builder's Corner

**Multi-Agent Claude Workflow Goes Viral** — Developer Boris Cherny shared a production workflow where [one Claude instance prompts and coordinates other Claude instances](https://x.com/thegreatest_sv/status/2063954742389121195), reportedly delivering 250% more coding output. The setup includes cron-scheduled agents that run autonomously—a glimpse of where AI development workflows are headed.

**Open-Source Mac AI Memory Framework** — [Kocoro](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2063943851291927024) launched as an open-source Mac framework that gives AI agents persistent memory of past sessions and interactions, addressing the context window limitations that plague current AI workflows.
