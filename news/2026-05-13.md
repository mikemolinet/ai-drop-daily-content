**OpenAI Launches Daybreak Cybersecurity Initiative**

Sam Altman announced yesterday that OpenAI is launching Daybreak, a new effort focused on accelerating cyber defense and continuous software security. The initiative aims to partner with companies to help them leverage AI for ongoing security monitoring and threat detection.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**OpenAI Makes Major Cybersecurity Play with Daybreak Initiative**

OpenAI is betting big on AI-powered cybersecurity with [Daybreak](https://x.com/sama/status/2053951874408276193), a new initiative announced by Sam Altman yesterday. The program focuses on "accelerating cyber defense and continuously securing software" by partnering directly with companies to implement AI-driven security systems.

This comes at a critical moment as AI capabilities in cybersecurity reach what Altman calls a "threshold." The timing suggests OpenAI sees both offensive and defensive AI capabilities accelerating rapidly, and wants to ensure they're on the right side of that arms race. For enterprise customers, this could mean much more proactive threat detection and automated response systems.

The announcement was light on technical details, but the focus on "continuous" security suggests real-time monitoring and response capabilities that go beyond traditional periodic scans.

**Tailwind CSS Ships v4.3 with New Utility Classes**

Adam Wathan [announced Tailwind CSS v4.3](https://x.com/adamwathan/status/2054252541722919183) yesterday, adding scrollbar utilities, zoom utilities, and tab-size utilities alongside other improvements. While incremental, this continues Tailwind's strategy of expanding their utility-first approach to cover more CSS properties.

For builders, the scrollbar utilities are particularly useful given the ongoing challenges of cross-browser scrollbar styling. The zoom utilities could be valuable for responsive design patterns and accessibility implementations.

According to [new metrics](https://x.com/__Aneef/status/2054496907817374107), 250,000 daily active on-chain agents are now running, with 68% of new DeFi protocols shipping with autonomous AI agents. This indicates the agentic economy is moving from experiment to infrastructure.

**Near Protocol Building Privacy Layer for AI Agents**
