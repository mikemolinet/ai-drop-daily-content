The AI safety conversation got presidential backing yesterday as OpenAI's Sam Altman [endorsed the new White House Executive Order](https://x.com/sama/status/2061973280655904815) on AI development, saying "the US should lead on AI by continuing to develop the very best models, making sure they're safe, and getting cyber tools into the hands of trusted defenders."

The timing coincides with a heated debate sparked by Ted Chiang's new Atlantic piece arguing that [AI is fundamentally not conscious](https://www.theatlantic.com/philosophy/2026/06/no-artificial-intelligence-is-not-conscious/687378/) and warning about "moral atrophy" if we delegate ethical decisions to machines. Ethan Mollick [pushed back](https://x.com/emollick/status/2062393081568076038), noting that "repeated randomized trials find AI is apparently a good ethicist" despite Chiang's concerns.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**Google Drops Gemma 4 12B with Multimodal Capabilities**

Google [launched Gemma 4 12B](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemma-4-12b/), claiming near-26B model performance in a smaller package. The unified, encoder-free multimodal model can handle text, images, and audio in a single architecture. Early tests show it running locally on a single RTX 4090, making high-end AI capabilities more accessible to developers who can't afford massive compute clusters.

**IBM and Google Cloud Launch Joint AI Practice**

This isn't just another partnership announcement. [IBM Consulting Advantage is now directly integrated](https://x.com/alphaticaio/status/2062484338772488402) with Google's Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, giving IBM's 160,000 consultants immediate access to deploy Google's AI agents. The move signals enterprise AI is moving from pilot projects to full-scale implementations with established consulting muscle behind it.

### 📡 Quick Hits

**Academic Performance Tanks with AI Usage**: UC Berkeley CS classes are seeing [failing grades soar](https://www.dailycal.org/news/campus/academics/failing-grades-soar-as-professors-see-greater-ai-usage-dwindling-math-skills-in-uc-berkeley/article_16fad0bf-02cb-4b8c-8d88-888ffd9f8608.html) as students rely on AI tools but lose fundamental math skills. The data suggests AI might be creating a generation of developers who can prompt but can't problem-solve.

**Microsoft Ships MDASH Security System**: Microsoft [announced MDASH](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/05/12/defense-at-ai-speed-microsofts-new-multi-model-agentic-security-system-tops-leading-industry-benchmark/), a multi-model agentic security scanning system that's topping industry benchmarks. The system uses multiple AI models working together to identify and respond to cyber threats at machine speed.

**Voice AI Gets Emotional Intelligence**: [Modulate's Velma platform](https://x.com/nijokestu/status/2062506741351461010) is tackling the "half the signal" problem in voice AI by preserving emotional context instead of stripping it away during speech-to-text conversion. The platform maintains hesitation, tension, and sarcasm that current LLMs miss.

**Y2 Challenges Bloomberg Terminal**: A new [live intelligence platform](https://x.com/heynavtoor/status/2062502325525385572) launched with 100+ OSINT feeds and 40+ AI models using a pay-per-request model instead of Bloomberg's $50K annual terminal fees. The USDC-based pricing targets both humans and AI agents.
