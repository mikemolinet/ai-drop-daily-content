# AI News Drop — Thursday, June 18, 2026

China's AI funding machine just hit a new gear, OpenAI is raiding Google's talent pool, and world models are officially a venture darling. Let's get into it.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**DeepSeek Raises $7.4 Billion — and Keeps Control**

Chinese AI lab DeepSeek has closed its first external funding round at CNY 50B+ (~$7.4B), in a deal structured to keep investors close but voting rights closer. The lab, which rattled Western AI circles earlier this year with its open-weight releases, is now capitalized at a level that puts it firmly in the same conversation as Anthropic and xAI. What makes this notable isn't just the number — it's the structure. DeepSeek apparently negotiated terms that preserve internal control, a move that signals the lab intends to stay aggressive and independent rather than getting absorbed into a larger Chinese tech conglomerate. Watch for accelerated model releases and compute buildout in H2 2026. The gap everyone said was "months" keeps looking shorter. [Via Digitimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260617VL211/deepsee)

**OpenAI Lands Noam Brown — Sam Altman Is Openly Giddy**

Sam Altman posted twice about it, so you know he means it: Noam Brown, one of the architects behind landmark AI reasoning breakthroughs (Libratus, Cicero), is joining OpenAI. Altman called him someone he's "wanted to work with since the very beginning of OpenAI." Brown is best known for solving complex multi-agent, incomplete-information games — exactly the kind of reasoning scaffolding that separates current frontier models from the next tier. With GPT-6 speculation intensifying and OpenAI needing to justify its stratospheric valuation, landing a researcher of Brown's caliber matters. Separately, there are reports that Google Gemini's co-lead engineer also just departed for OpenAI — a brain drain signal worth watching. [Altman's post](https://x.com/sama/status/2067427421083652131)

**Odyssey Raises $310M Series B for World Models**

[Odyssey](https://x.com/odysseyml) has closed a $310M Series B to build general world models — AI systems that can understand and simulate physical reality. Founded in 2023, the company is making a long-term bet that world models become a foundational layer, like LLMs did for language. The timing is interesting: this raise lands as agentic AI is pushing hard against the limits of language-only reasoning. If agents need to navigate real environments, world models stop being research and start being infrastructure. $310M says at least some LPs agree.
