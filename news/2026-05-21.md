**OpenAI Model Solves Major Math Conjecture**

AI just crossed a new intellectual frontier. An OpenAI model [disproved a central conjecture in discrete geometry](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/) that mathematicians have wrestled with for years — marking the first time a general-purpose AI has tackled a major open mathematical problem.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**OpenAI's Math Breakthrough Changes the Game**

This isn't just another benchmark win. OpenAI's model attacked the Erdős–Ko–Rado conjecture in discrete geometry, providing a formal disproof that human mathematicians are still validating. Sam Altman called it "a kinda big milestone" while admitting to "complicated feelings" about AI extending beyond human understanding.

The implications are massive. We're moving from AI that can solve known problems to AI that can discover new mathematical truths. This positions AI as a genuine research partner rather than just a powerful calculator. Watch for this to accelerate AI adoption in academic research and R&D-heavy industries.

**Google Pushes AI Search Into Revenue Mode**

Google [officially announced](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/google-marketing-live-search-ads/) that ads will now appear in AI Mode search results. The move signals Google's confidence that AI search is ready for mainstream monetization — and their urgency to protect search revenue from ChatGPT and Perplexity.

This fundamentally changes the AI search landscape. Free AI search was always unsustainable at scale, and Google just fired the starting gun on the revenue race. Expect other AI search players to follow with premium tiers and advertising models within months.

**A16z Leads Exa's $250M Search Infrastructure Round**

AI search infrastructure startup Exa raised [$250 million](https://www.pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/2026/exa-raises-250-million-for-ai-powered-search-infrastructure/) at a $2.2B valuation, with a16z leading the Series C. The timing isn't coincidental — as AI agents proliferate, they need better ways to find and retrieve information from the web.

Exa is building search specifically for AI agents, not humans. Traditional search engines optimize for human browsing behavior, but agents need structured, reliable data retrieval. This could become critical infrastructure as the agent economy scales.
