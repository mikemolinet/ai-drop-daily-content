### 🔥 The Big Stories

**OpenAI Cuts GPT-5.6 Luna Pricing by 80%**

Sam Altman [announced today](https://x.com/sama/status/2082880720989532597) a sweeping price cut across the GPT-5.6 lineup: Luna drops 80% to $0.20/M input and $1.20/M output tokens. Terra gets a 20% trim to $2/$12. And Sol now has a "Fast mode" — up to 2.5x the speed for 2x the price, same intelligence.

The Luna cut is the headline. $0.20 per million tokens puts frontier-class inference within reach of projects that couldn't justify the cost a month ago. For builders, this isn't incremental — it changes the unit economics of products built on AI API calls. Apps that were marginally profitable or required aggressive caching strategies now have more room.

The pattern here is consistent: OpenAI prices drop aggressively whenever competitive pressure intensifies. Reuters noted this week that inexpensive Chinese models are gaining ground on OpenAI and Anthropic on their home turf. Coincidence? Unlikely.

**ByteDance Ships Seedance 2.5 — A Cinematic Video Model Goes Global**

ByteDance's seed team [launched Seedance 2.5 globally today](https://x.com/Scobleizer/status/2083093737362821347), with Dreamina AI as the consumer-facing product. The pitch: long-form storytelling, multimodal input, cinematic output quality.

Video generation has been a crowded field, but ByteDance has infrastructure advantages and a massive distribution base. Seedance 2.5 positions squarely against Sora and Runway — and the "global launch" framing suggests this isn't a soft release. If the quality holds up to the marketing, this is a meaningful entrant.

Watch for how this intersects with TikTok's content ecosystem. ByteDance building the best video AI _and_ the biggest video distribution platform is a moat that's hard to replicate.
