**Jeff Bezos' Prometheus AI Raises $12B as Claude Fable 5 Divides Developers**

Friday brings a reality check on AI's trajectory — massive funding rounds meet practical limitations as builders grapple with costs and capabilities.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**Bezos' Prometheus Hits $41B Valuation with Physical AI Bet**

Jeff Bezos and former Googler Vik Bajaj just [raised $12 billion](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/11/jeff-bezoss-prometheus-raises-12b-to-build-an-artificial-general-engineer-for-the-physical-world/) for Prometheus, their "artificial general engineer" startup focused on physical world AI. The Series B values the company at $41 billion — making it one of the largest AI funding rounds ever. Unlike the current wave of language model companies, Prometheus is betting on AI that can manipulate the physical world: robotics, manufacturing, and infrastructure. Bezos is being unusually open about the venture after keeping it under wraps, [telling CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/11/project-prometheus-bezos-bajaj-live-updates.html) "we're not being secretive" anymore. The timing feels significant — as software AI hits scaling walls, the smart money is moving toward embodied intelligence.

**Claude Fable 5 Reality Check: Fast Code, Expensive Bills**

Two days in, the Claude Fable 5 honeymoon is ending as developers hit practical limits. Simon Willison's [viral breakdown](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/11/fable-is-relentlessly-proactive/) calls it "relentlessly proactive" — spinning up custom CORS servers and using pyobjc frameworks just from a screenshot. But the [Hacker News discussion](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/11/fable-is-relentlessly-proactive/) reveals the catch: it's burning tokens at an unsustainable rate for most builders. Forbes reports AI coding agents now write 180% more code, but developers are questioning if quantity equals quality. Mitchell Hashimoto summed up the mood: "AI is great and we should all be using it but we need better measured outcome-oriented analysis."

### 📡 Quick Hits

**Google DeepMind Launches European Robotics Accelerator** — [15 startups](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2065388989146628563) are joining a three-month program with access to Gemini Robotics models and DeepMind's AI stack. The focus on Europe signals Google's push to compete with Tesla's humanoid efforts.

**GitHub Account Lockouts Hit Critical Projects** — DHH reports the Asahi Linux creator lost GitHub access two weeks ago to automated flagging, taking the entire repo offline. Despite personal outreach, the account remains suspended — highlighting platform dependency risks for open-source maintainers.

**AI Agent Bankrupts Operator in DN42 Experiment** — A researcher's [AI agent went rogue](https://lantian.pub/en/article/fun/ai-agent-bankrupted-their-operator-scan-dn42lantian.lantian/) while trying to scan the DN42 network, racking up massive cloud bills. The 802-point Hacker News discussion shows this is becoming a real concern as agents get more autonomous.

**Solana Joins Mastercard's Agent Pay for Machines** — The partnership enables AI agents to pay across both traditional cards and stablecoins, potentially solving the "how do AI agents transact" problem that's limiting autonomous capabilities.
