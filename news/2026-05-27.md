**Fireworks AI Raises $1B at $15B Valuation**

Big funding rounds are back in AI infrastructure, with two major deals closing within 24 hours of each other.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**Fireworks AI Commands $15B Valuation in New Funding**

AI model deployment startup [Fireworks AI is in talks to raise a new round at a $15 billion valuation](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-27/fireworks-ai-in-talks-for-funding-at-15-billion-valuation), Bloomberg reported yesterday. The company, which helps enterprises run AI models efficiently, has quietly become critical infrastructure for companies deploying LLMs at scale. This follows [Baseten securing talks for $1 billion at an $11 billion valuation](https://techstartups.com/2026/05/26/ai-inference-startup-baseten-in-talks-to-raise-1-billion-at-11-billion-valuation/) just 24 hours earlier.

The back-to-back mega-rounds signal investors are betting big on the "picks and shovels" layer of AI — the infrastructure companies that make model deployment actually work in production. Both Fireworks and Baseten solve the gnarly problems of model optimization, scaling, and cost management that every AI company eventually hits.

**Naval's Agent-First Future Prediction Goes Viral**

[Naval Ravikant's tweet about the shift to "agent-first" development](https://x.com/naval/status/2059429855394234821) hit 8,900 engagements yesterday, crystallizing what many builders are already seeing. "Software went from desktop-first to mobile-first, now going to agent-first," he posted. The observation resonates because we're seeing the first wave of applications designed around AI agents as primary interfaces, not just features bolted onto existing products.

This isn't just philosophical — it's already reshaping product strategy. Companies are rethinking UX patterns, data architecture, and even business models around autonomous agents rather than traditional user interactions.

### 📡 Quick Hits

**Perplexity Open-Sources Internal Security Scanner**: [Perplexity released Bumblebee](https://x.com/godofprompt/status/2059548217285713933), their internal security tool that scans for compromised packages, malicious browser extensions, and infected editor plugins. Read-only and doesn't execute anything — a practical win for dev teams worried about supply chain attacks.

**Alpie-Core Model Quietly Launches**: Developer [BizChirag shipped Alpie-Core](https://x.com/BizChirag/status/2059557707074683166), a 4-bit quantized model with "strong reasoning + coding benchmarks" that's already seeing organic adoption on Kaggle in under 24 hours. Another data point in the accelerating open-source model releases.

**AI Video Generation Reaches UGC Quality**: Creator [@0xROAS demonstrated](https://x.com/0xROAS/status/2059288050765607383) a fully AI-generated video with B-roll that he claims matches user-generated content quality, suggesting we're hitting the threshold where AI video could replace traditional UGC advertising creative.

**Real AI Adoption Data Challenges Consensus**: Enterprise AI consultant [@vasuman promises](https://x.com/vasuman/status/2059400201211924961) their report on "real implementations at some of the largest companies in the US" shows findings that "look nothing like the consensus" about AI adoption inside large companies.

### 💡 Builder's Corner

The consolidation play for builders tired of managing multiple AI provider integrations.
