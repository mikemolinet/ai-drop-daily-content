The most under-reported story in AI just happened. Anthropic quietly acquired [Stainless](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-stainless), the company behind auto-generated SDKs for major AI APIs. This isn't just a talent grab — it's a play for the infrastructure layer that every AI company desperately needs but nobody wants to build.

**Why this matters:** Stainless generates the official SDKs for OpenAI, Anthropic, and dozens of other AI companies. When you \`pip install openai\`, you're using Stainless-generated code. Now Anthropic owns the toolchain that makes APIs actually usable. This could be the most strategically important acquisition of 2026 that nobody's talking about.

**The timing is perfect.** As [AI agents become economic actors](https://x.com/VitriBoost/status/2056691126757736544) (AEON just raised $8M betting on this), the API layer becomes critical infrastructure. Anthropic just bought a massive competitive moat.
