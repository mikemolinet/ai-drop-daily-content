OpenAI just rewrote the rules with Microsoft — and it's bigger than the surface suggests.

## 🔥 The Big Stories

**OpenAI Breaks Free from Microsoft's Exclusive Cloud Deal**

Sam Altman [announced yesterday](https://x.com/sama/status/2048755148361707946) that OpenAI has "updated" its partnership with Microsoft — corporate speak for ending one of the most important exclusive deals in AI. While Microsoft remains OpenAI's primary cloud partner through 2032, OpenAI can now sell across all clouds. The revenue sharing that helped fund OpenAI's early development is also ending.

The implications are massive. Microsoft invested $13 billion partly for exclusive access to OpenAI's models and cloud compute lock-in. Now that exclusivity is gone, even as Microsoft retains rights to AGI models through 2032. This suggests OpenAI feels confident enough to give up guaranteed revenue for flexibility — or that they needed escape hatches as competition intensifies. Watch how quickly OpenAI moves to Google Cloud and AWS deals.

**Anthropic Bay Area Home Listed for Company Stock Only**

A $4.85 million Bay Area home is [now for sale with a twist](https://x.com/TheGeorgePu/status/2048857756749218100): cash not accepted, Anthropic shares only. The seller, reportedly a banker, specifically wants a 28-year-old Anthropic engineer who has equity but limited liquidity. It's a perfect encapsulation of how pre-IPO AI wealth is creating new market dynamics.

This isn't just a quirky real estate story — it signals how illiquid AI wealth is reshaping Bay Area economics. When equity becomes currency before it's actually liquid, you get market distortions that could get messy if valuations shift. The fact that someone is willing to bet their home's value on Anthropic's private valuation shows just how much confidence (or desperation) exists around AI company prospects.

## 📡 Quick Hits

**DeepSeek's Pricing Undercuts Everyone**: Developer feedback shows [DeepSeek offering tokens 35x cheaper](https://x.com/thdxr/status/2048991814183473302) than Claude Opus, with cached tokens 178x cheaper during their current 75% discount. That's not just competitive pricing — it's potentially loss-leader territory to grab market share.

**Instacart Founder's AI Hedge Fund Goes Live**: Apoorva Mehta [launched Abundance](https://x.com/linasbeliunas/status/2049059667771301931), an AI-first hedge fund where the portfolio manager is a swarm of AI agents, not humans. The Palo Alto firm represents a new category of fully autonomous investment management.

**Claude Code vs Opus Benchmarks**: New [BridgeBench hallucination scores](https://x.com/bridgebench/status/2049095178732941442) show Claude Opus 4.7 ranking #5 (77.1 score) while GPT-5.5 sits at #13 (73.6 score). GPT-5.5 might be smarter, but Claude remains more trustworthy for production use.

**TON Launches Agentic Wallets**: TON Tech [shipped Agentic Wallets](https://x.com/Cointelegraph/status/2049101388563558827) on Telegram, letting AI agents hold funds and transact on-chain without human approval for each step. This removes a major friction point for autonomous agent commerce.

**Stanford PhD Beaten by GPT Pro**: A 23-year-old using GPT Pro [reportedly cracked](https://x.com/TheGeorgePu/status/2048902548279443630) a major math problem that a Stanford PhD spent four years working on. If true, it's another data point for AI's accelerating capability in formal reasoning domains.
