Monday morning and we're already seeing the global AI infrastructure scale in real-time. Sam Altman [announced](https://x.com/sama/status/2056165722804654196) that ChatGPT Images 2.0 has generated over 1 billion images in India alone, marking a massive adoption milestone for OpenAI's latest visual AI capabilities.

## 🔥 The Big Stories

**OpenAI's India Milestone Reveals Global AI Adoption Pattern**

The 1 billion image milestone in India isn't just a vanity metric — it's a signal of how AI tools are being adopted outside the traditional Silicon Valley bubble. India's massive developer and creator population is clearly embracing multimodal AI at scale. This follows the broader pattern we're seeing where AI adoption is happening faster in markets with younger, more tech-forward populations. Watch for OpenAI to use this data to justify further international expansion and localized AI models.

**Anthropic Ships 2-Hour Claude Agent Training Course**

Anthropic [released](https://x.com/CryptoTony__/status/2056330707991953789) a comprehensive 2-hour training program on building Claude agents, hosted by the engineer who built Claude Code. This is smart positioning — instead of just shipping tools, they're actively educating developers on best practices. The course covers everything from basic agent setup to advanced self-managing systems, suggesting Anthropic sees agent development as a key differentiator in the AI platform wars.

**AI Startup Recursive Superintelligence Raises $650M at $4.65B Valuation**

[Recursive Superintelligence closed a massive $650 million round](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/05/279726-ai-startup-recursive-superintelligence-secures-650m/) led by GV and Greycroft, hitting a $4.65 billion valuation. The company is building AI systems that can recursively improve themselves — essentially working on the holy grail of AI research. This round size puts them in the same funding league as OpenAI and Anthropic, signaling that investors believe we're still in the early innings of foundational AI model development.
