**Apple Ships Claude.md Files by Accident, Anthropic Drops Claude Security Beta**

Friday's serving up some spicy security drama. Apple just hotfixed their Support app after accidentally shipping internal Claude documentation, while Anthropic launched a new security tool that's already causing vendor drama.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**Apple's Oops Moment Confirms Claude Integration**

Apple pushed a [hotfix to their Support app](https://twitter.com/IntCyberDigest/status/2050163580339667073) within hours of v5.13 going live — all to remove Claude.md files that weren't supposed to ship. The speed of the fix (we're talking same-day turnaround) confirms this wasn't intentional. While Apple's been quiet about AI partnerships, finding Claude documentation in production code is about as close to confirmation as you get. Watch for more Apple-Anthropic integration announcements in the coming months.

**Anthropic Launches Claude Security, Immediately Creates Competitor Drama**

[Claude Security just hit public beta](https://twitter.com/WesRoth/status/2050153263040016450) for Enterprise customers, using Opus 4.7 to autonomously scan codebases for vulnerabilities. The twist? Multiple developers are reporting that [Claude Code refuses requests or charges extra](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40242847) when your commits mention "OpenClaw" (a competing AI coding tool). If true, this suggests Anthropic is using AI to detect and potentially throttle competitor usage — a concerning precedent for the "neutral AI tools" narrative.

**OpenAI's GPT-5.5 Matches Anthropic's Mythos on Cyber Tasks**

Fresh benchmarks show [GPT-5.5 hitting 71.4% vs Mythos's 68.6%](https://twitter.com/Hesamation/status/2049971981886312463) on expert cybersecurity challenges. More impressive: GPT-5.5 solved in 10 minutes what took human experts 12 hours, for $1.73 in compute costs. After OpenAI criticized Anthropic for limiting Mythos access, they're now [restricting their own Cyber model](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/after-dissing-anthropic-for-limiting-mythos-openai-restricts-access-to-cyber-too/) to critical infrastructure teams only.

### 📡 Quick Hits

**Google DeepMind's AI Co-Clinician Outperforms Doctors**: [New research](https://twitter.com/2xnmore/status/2050153260515045817) shows their AI system beating real doctors and GPT-5.4 in blind evaluations across 98 primary care queries. Healthcare AI just got serious validation.

**Malware Hits PyTorch Lightning**: Security researchers found [Shai-Hulud themed malware](https://semgrep.dev/blog/2026/malicious-dependency-in-pytorch-lightning-used-for-ai-training/) in the popular AI training library. If you're using PyTorch Lightning, update immediately.

**Mistral Ships Remote Agents**: [Vibe now supports remote coding agents](https://mistral.ai/news/vibe-remote-agents-mistral-medium-3-5) powered by Mistral Medium 3.5, plus a new Work mode in Le Chat for complex tasks.

**Legal AI Gets $600M**: Stockholm's [Legora raised a massive Series D](https://techfundingnews.com/legora-600m-series-d-nvidia-atlassian-legal-ai-5-6b-valuation/) led by Nvidia and Atlassian at a $5.6B valuation for legal AI tools.
