**OpenRouter Fusion API Combines Multiple Models, Beats Any Solo Performance**

Monday morning brings a fascinating development in model composition: OpenRouter just launched their Fusion API, which combines multiple AI models into compound systems that outperform any individual model.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**OpenRouter Ships Model Fusion, Beats All Solo Benchmarks**

OpenRouter's new [Fusion API](https://openrouter.ai/openrouter/fusion) lets developers combine multiple models into single compound systems — and the results are striking. The Fable 5 + GPT-5.5 fusion scored 93/100 on their benchmark suite, while a three-way fusion of Opus 4.8 + GPT-5.5 + Gemini 3.1 Pro outscored every individual model including the latest releases.

This isn't just ensemble voting — it's intelligent routing and collaboration between models during inference. The implications for production AI systems could be huge, especially for complex tasks where different models excel at different aspects. The challenge will be cost and latency, but for high-value use cases where accuracy trumps speed, this could become the new standard.

**Local LLM Economics Hit Sweet Spot in 2026**

The local AI hardware landscape has reached an interesting inflection point. A used RTX 3090 (24GB) plus basic PC components now costs $1000-1500 total, enough to run capable models like Qwen3.6-27B for agents and tool use, or Nex-N2-Mini for deeper reasoning tasks. For 16GB setups, Gemma 4-12B delivers solid all-around performance.

What's notable is how the performance-per-dollar curve has shifted. These aren't toy models — they're production-capable systems that can handle real workloads without API costs. For builders doing heavy AI development, the ROI calculation is increasingly compelling.
