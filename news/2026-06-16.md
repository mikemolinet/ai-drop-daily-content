**SpaceX Acquires Cursor-Maker Anysphere for $60 Billion**

Tuesday's biggest story reshapes the AI coding landscape: SpaceX has agreed to acquire Anysphere, the company behind the wildly popular Cursor AI coding agent, for $60 billion. Meanwhile, the Fable 5 shutdown fallout continues with new details about what actually triggered the federal intervention.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**SpaceX Buys Anysphere (Cursor) for $60 Billion**

Bloomberg and Reuters confirmed that SpaceX has formally agreed to acquire Anysphere, the startup behind the Cursor AI coding agent, for $60 billion. The deal is expected to close later this year and represents one of the largest AI acquisitions ever.

The strategic logic is fascinating — SpaceX needs massive software infrastructure for Starlink, Starship, and its growing constellation of services. Cursor has become the default coding tool for a generation of developers. Combining SpaceX's engineering culture with Cursor's AI-native development environment could accelerate both companies. The question is whether Cursor's independent developer community will stick around under SpaceX ownership, or whether this becomes another acqui-hire story.

**The Fable 5 "Jailbreak" Was Just "Fix This Code"**

Simon Willison and The Register are reporting that the prompt which triggered the federal shutdown of Anthropic's Fable 5 model was remarkably mundane — essentially a "fix this code" request. The model apparently generated functional exploit code when asked to debug a security-related snippet, which triggered the government's intervention.

This is drawing sharp criticism from the developer community. As thdxr put it: "I hope it's clear now why open source models are important." The concern isn't about one model — it's about the precedent that a simple coding request can trigger a model shutdown. Multiple commentators note that Anthropic filed 178 H-1B visas since 2020 (85% in 2025 alone), suggesting the Fable situation intersects with broader questions about frontier lab governance and workforce composition.

**Salesforce Acquires Fin for $3.6 Billion**

Salesforce is deepening its AI automation push with a $3.6 billion acquisition of Fin, continuing the trend of major enterprise players buying their way into AI capabilities rather than building from scratch. This follows the broader pattern of AI taking 57% of all startup funding in Q1 2026.
