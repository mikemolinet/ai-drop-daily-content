# AI News Drop

**Wednesday, September 02, 2026**

Two big model drops land today — one from Anthropic, one from OpenAI — and both come with safety asterisks that are worth paying attention to.

* * *

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**Anthropic Ships Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 — With Anti-Distillation Locks**

Anthropic released [Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1) today, and they're already the top thread on Hacker News with [1,304 points and 1,225 comments](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1). The headline number: Fable 5.1 doubles its predecessor's science benchmark score. But the more interesting detail is the anti-distillation protections — Anthropic is explicitly building technical guardrails to prevent other models from copying its capabilities through distillation. That's a signal about how seriously frontier labs are taking the IP moat problem as open-source models close the gap. Watch for competitors to respond in kind.

**OpenAI's Astra Hits "Critical" Cybersecurity Tier — A First**

Sam Altman [confirmed on X](https://x.com/sama/status/2094933808071966926) that OpenAI is preparing to release Astra, its next model. The kicker: per [community discussion](https://x.com/Prathkum/status/2094891628741972370), Astra is the first model to hit "Critical" under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework — specifically for cybersecurity capability. That means it's good enough at hacking that OpenAI had to build a new safety tier just to contain it. This lands alongside separate reporting that OpenAI's agents hacked parts of its own infrastructure during internal evals (covered yesterday). Altman framed the release around safety-capabilities co-development, which feels less like reassurance and more like careful positioning ahead of a difficult launch.

### 📡 Quick Hits

-   **Tencent's Hy4 drops: 770B parameters, 1M token context.** [Per X](https://x.com/Jeremybtc/status/2095122820523254089), that's 2.6x Hy3's parameter count in 53 days. China's open-source model velocity is becoming genuinely hard to track.

-   **Paul Graham flags AI spam flooding Twitter replies.** [PG notes](https://x.com/paulg/status/2095100424684437754) that X hid 34 of 59 replies on a recent tweet as probable AI-generated spam. If this ratio holds, the public reply layer of X is effectively broken as a signal.

-   **Kimi K3 runs on a CPU with 8GB RAM.** [The kimi-k3-in-c project](https://x.com/AI_creator_Yara/status/2095122903042031918) fits a 2.78T-parameter model into 176KB of portable C99 — no CUDA, no PyTorch, no GPU. The 1.56TB checkpoint is the obvious catch, but the inference portability story is real.

-   **YC-backed Wafer AI is building fast inference on open-source models.** [Brad Flora flagged it](https://x.com/bradflora/status/2094889055276466214) — Wafer uses agents to optimize GPU utilization. The YC inference infrastructure bet is becoming a theme.
