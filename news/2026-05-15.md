**Codex Hits Mobile as OpenAI Pushes 30-Day Free Switch Deal**

OpenAI is betting big that Codex can poach enterprise customers from GitHub Copilot and Cursor — and they're putting serious money behind it.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**OpenAI Goes Full-Court Press on Codex Adoption**

Sam Altman [announced yesterday](https://x.com/sama/status/2054626219858293128) that any company wanting to switch from their current AI coding tool gets two months of free Codex usage for the next 30 days. This isn't a gentle product trial — it's an aggressive land-grab move that screams "we think we can convert your entire dev team in 60 days." The timing pairs with Codex [finally hitting ChatGPT mobile](https://x.com/sama/status/2055034461591588916), though some developers are [confused about what's actually new](https://x.com/adamwathan/status/2055037124173263269) since beta access has been rolling for months.

The mobile play is smart. Developers are increasingly coding on iPads and wanting flexibility beyond the desktop. But the free switch program is the real story — OpenAI is essentially subsidizing customer acquisition at scale, betting they can prove ROI fast enough that teams won't switch back when the free period ends.

**A16z Opens First International Office in Japan**

Andreessen Horowitz is [setting up shop in Japan](https://x.com/andrewchen/status/2054988896845656374) this summer — their first office outside the US. This isn't just geographic expansion; it's a clear signal they see Japan as the next major AI hub outside Silicon Valley and China. With Japan's recent AI investment push and regulatory clarity, plus strong robotics/hardware foundations, a16z is positioning early for what they clearly believe will be a significant market. Expect other major VCs to follow quickly.
