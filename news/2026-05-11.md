**Sam Altman Teases "Goblin" Model as AI Politics Disrupts LA Elections**

The weekend brought unexpected signals from OpenAI's CEO and the first glimpse of AI fundamentally reshaping electoral politics.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**OpenAI's Sam Altman Floats "Goblin" for Next Model Name**

Sam Altman's weekend [Twitter spree](https://x.com/sama/status/2053572868936761350) included an unexpected crowdsource moment: "what if we name the next model 'goblin'... almost worth it to make you all happy." The tweet racked up nearly 13K engagements, suggesting the AI community's appetite for less corporate naming conventions. While likely tongue-in-cheek, it comes as OpenAI faces mounting pressure to differentiate in an increasingly crowded model landscape. Altman also dropped a cryptic follow-up about "leveragemaxxing," hinting at potential efficiency gains in upcoming releases.

**AI-Generated Content Drives First Election Victory**

Los Angeles is witnessing what [Andrew Chen calls](https://x.com/andrewchen/status/2053660266806779945) "the world's first political election determined by AI-created viral meme videos." The development marks a watershed moment where AI content creation has moved beyond marketing gimmicks to actual electoral influence. Historically, campaign dollars flowed to traditional media buys, but AI-generated social content is proving more cost-effective and viral. This could be a preview of 2028 dynamics nationwide.

**Anthropic Ships Free Claude Masterclass**

Anthropic [released a 24-minute workshop](https://x.com/AiwithZoaina/status/2053777344545067289) teaching advanced Claude prompting techniques, delivered by their internal team. The move signals growing competition in developer education as companies race to build sticky user bases beyond just model performance. Free, high-quality training content could become a key differentiation strategy as prompt engineering skills become table stakes for technical teams.

### 📡 Quick Hits

**Video AI Tools Get Major Updates**: AI video platform Viggle [added support](https://x.com/damengchen/status/2053718815016689885) for Seedance 2.0, Veo 3.1, and Grok's Imagine, showcasing the rapid pace of video generation improvements.

**TON Launches AI-First Smart Contracts**: The Open Network [shipped Acton](https://x.com/creptosolutions/status/2053804795773817057), a toolkit designed specifically for AI agents like ChatGPT and Claude to interact with blockchain infrastructure.

**GPU Efficiency Breakthrough**: Sakana AI and NVIDIA [released TwELL](https://x.com/Marktechpost/status/2053762696500121658), new CUDA kernels that exploit sparsity in LLM feedforward layers, potentially cutting compute costs for 80%+ of model operations.

**Payment Rails for AI Agents**: Payments startup Yellow [positioned itself](https://x.com/Yellow/status/2053802549287760374) as the "PayPal moment for AI," building infrastructure for autonomous agent transactions.
