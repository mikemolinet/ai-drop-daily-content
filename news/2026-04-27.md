**Sam Altman Calls for OS Redesign as Coding Agents Drive Developer Behavior Shift**

The race to rebuild computing for AI is accelerating. While developers report fundamental changes in how they work, platform leaders are questioning whether our entire stack needs rethinking.

### 🔥 The Big Stories

**Sam Altman: Time to Redesign Operating Systems for AI**

OpenAI's CEO [dropped a thread yesterday](https://x.com/sama/status/2048428561481265539) calling for serious reconsideration of how operating systems and user interfaces are designed. His key insight: "there should be a protocol that is equally usable by people and agents." This comes as Altman noted developers switching to polyphasic sleep schedules because GPT-5.5's coding capabilities are so advanced they "can't afford to be sleeping for such long stretches and miss out on working." The comments reflect a broader pattern — AI tools aren't just augmenting workflows, they're forcing fundamental behavior changes that existing interfaces weren't designed for.

**"The West Forgot How to Code" Goes Viral Among Developers**

A post arguing that AI-assisted developers "ship faster but understand nothing" is gaining traction in dev communities, according to [Greg Isenberg's observations](https://x.com/gregisenberg/status/2048411388591087771). The thesis: the next generation will be "illiterate at the layer that matters." Isenberg notes this panic happens every decade — assembly devs worried about C developers, C devs about higher-level languages. But the speed of this transition feels different. Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch [countered that coding agents](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2048523195305902341) will be "the foundation of all superintelligence," arguing that coding ability equals computer proficiency itself.

**Instacart Founder Launches Fully Autonomous AI Hedge Fund**

Apoorva Mehta has launched an AI hedge fund where [thousands of AI agents](https://x.com/aaditsh/status/2048022122795294980) handle the entire investment process — picking stocks, sizing bets, executing trades with "no humans in the loop." The move signals a broader trend of founders applying AI-first thinking to traditional industries. What's notable is the timing: Mehta started building this after seeing AI's rapid advancement in other domains, suggesting a wave of similar "AI-native" financial services may be coming.

### 📡 Quick Hits

**Evan Spiegel: "Software Is Not a Moat" Lesson Applies to AI** — The Snapchat CEO [reminded everyone](https://x.com/lennysan/status/2048518815739527554) that the software-as-moat lesson from 15 years ago is something "everyone is discovering today with AI." Distribution and user behavior remain the real defensible assets.

**Frontend Design "Basically Solved"** — Multiple developers are reporting that Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5 have reached the point where [frontend design is essentially automated](https://x.com/EXM7777/status/2048423367309766967). If true, this represents a major inflection point for web development workflows.

**Binance Ships Keyless Wallet for AI Agents** — The exchange [launched Agentic Wallet](https://x.com/Cryptic_Web3/status/2048732139160555708), allowing AI agents to trade and manage crypto across multiple chains without traditional key management. This infrastructure could accelerate autonomous trading systems.

**Paul Graham on Model Company Fears** — YC's founder [offered perspective](https://x.com/paulg/status/2048677984106664227) on startup anxiety about being "eaten" by model companies: "You're far more likely simply to fail." The reminder that execution still matters, even in the AI age.

### 💡 Builder's Corner

**Chrome's Prompt API Goes Live** — Google shipped the [Prompt API](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/ai/prompt-api) for Chrome, giving web developers direct access to on-device AI models. This could be huge for privacy-conscious applications that need AI features without cloud dependencies.

**EvanFlow: TDD for Claude Code** — A new [open-source tool](https://github.com/evanklem/evanflow) creates test-driven development feedback loops specifically for Claude Code projects. Early reports suggest it significantly improves AI-generated code quality by forcing iterative testing cycles.
